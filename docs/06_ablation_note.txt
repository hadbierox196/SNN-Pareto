To address potential parameter confounds, we trained a capacity-constrained
ANN baseline (105,866 parameters, 7.8x fewer than the full ANN) with
identical training configuration, achieving 91.61% +/- 0.08% test accuracy
at 5138.5 nJ per inference. The SNN at T*=8 achieves 89.69% accuracy at
3910.1 nJ -- lower accuracy at 1.3x lower energy -- confirming that the
SNN efficiency advantage is not an artifact of parameter count reduction.
